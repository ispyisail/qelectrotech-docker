> **Read that last row carefully.** The 22 counts every class whose effect is
> not `edit-properties`/`edit-text`, which includes the **16 classes that have
> no double-click handler at all**. Those are not outliers — they are items you
> cannot double-click. The genuinely interesting figure is the **4 classes that
> navigate**:
>
> | Class | Effect |
> |---|---|
> | `CrossRefItem` | navigate |
> | `ElementTextItemGroup` | navigate |
> | `DynamicElementTextItem` | edit-text + navigate |
> | `TerminalStripItem` | navigate + other |
>
> All four are **cross-reference displays** — rendered "where else does this
> appear" information — rather than editable objects. Every editable object in
> the audit resolves to `edit-properties` or `edit-text`. That is the convention
> PR #701 collided with: `ReportElement` is an editable object
> (`inherited(QetGraphicsItem)` -> `edit-properties`), so giving it navigation
> takes the editing gesture away.
